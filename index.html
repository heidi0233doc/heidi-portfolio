Heidi's Works

writer-director / producer /creator

I'm Heidi. My journey into filmmaking began after watching Agnès Varda's The Beaches of Agnès, and I have now spent over a decade working as a writer-director and producer. My time with diverse organizations, including Wainao under Radio Free Asia, Condé Nast, and Greenpeace, has enriched my creative practice with versatility and inclusivity. I have a passion for documentary filmmaking, yet I am equally adept at commercial and branded storytelling. From the subtle nuances of fashion, culture, and gender, to the raw realities of human rights, politics, and the environment, I always strive to look through the lens to uncover the most honest, deeply moving stories.

我是 Heidi。因為看了安妮·華達的《沙灘上的安妮》而決定開始拍片，至今從事影像編導、製作人已十多年。過去在美國自由亞洲電台（歪腦）、康泰納仕、綠色和平等跨領域團隊的經歷，讓我的創作養分既多元又包容。我喜歡拍紀錄片，也擅長為品牌說故事。從時尚、文化、性別的細膩流動，到人權、政治、環境等硬核議題，我都期許自己能透過鏡頭，挖掘出最觸動人心、最真實的故事。

Taiwan Unfiltered

News Magazine — TaiwanPlus
導演、製作人、剪輯作品

《Taiwan: Unfiltered》是一檔以人物紀實與社會觀察交織的當代新聞雜誌節目，聚焦台灣年輕世代在轉型期中的真實處境與自我摸索。節目從生活邊緣與制度裂縫出發，記錄台灣這一代「未完成但正在前進」的生命風景。

Taiwan: Unfiltered is a contemporary news magazine series capturing the raw, overlooked experiences of Taiwan's MZ generation. Through the lenses of politics, work, housing, and identity, the show follows Millennials and Gen Z as they question inherited norms, redefine success, and navigate the contradictions of a rapidly changing society.
