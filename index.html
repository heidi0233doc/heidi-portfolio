About · 關於

10+ Years of
Visual Storytelling

Based in Taiwan, I am a filmmaker and content creator specializing in documentary storytelling and high-quality visual production. With over a decade of experience, I focus on the intersection of fashion, culture, gender issues, and human rights — crafting narratives that are both aesthetically refined and socially engaged.

My expertise ranges from concept development to final post-production, delivering cinematic content for both traditional broadcast and digital platforms. Previous clients include Kering, Gucci, Chanel, Omega, IWC, Tommy Hilfiger, and Eva Air.

我是Heidi，擁有十多年影音創作經驗，擅長從企劃、腳本、拍攝到後製的全流程製作，作品橫跨紀錄片、品牌影片、議題報導、MV與Podcast，風格融合深度報導與美學敘事。
